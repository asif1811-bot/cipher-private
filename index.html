Security & Privacy

Your Privacy is
Our Architecture

Every interaction with Cipher Private is engineered with the security frameworks trusted by the most privacy-conscious institutions in the world. We are not merely compliant — we are exemplary.

Proudly Australian — Not UK. Not US. Not anywhere else.
AES-256 Document Encryption
All documents uploaded to your Cipher Vault are encrypted at rest and in transit using military-grade AES-256 encryption. Your files are never stored in plain text.
OTP-Secured Document Sharing
Send sensitive documents to any verified recipient via one-time passcode authentication. Every share is logged, time-stamped, and auditable.
Australian Privacy Act Compliant
We operate under the Privacy Act 1988 and Australian Privacy Principles. Your data is stored on Australian soil and never shared with third parties.
End-to-End Encrypted Chat
All communication through the Cipher Portal is fully encrypted. Your requests, conversations, and service history are visible only to you and your designated lifestyle manager.
