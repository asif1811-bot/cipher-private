Sydney's Most Exclusive Restaurants and Private Members Clubs in 2025

By Consiere·7 min read·Updated May 2026

Sydney has quietly become one of the world's great food cities. Access to the best tables requires either a long waitlist, the right connections, or a concierge who knows how to navigate both.

Sydney's Best Fine Dining

Quay

Consistently among the world's best restaurants, overlooking the Opera House and Harbour Bridge. Chef Peter Gilmore's tasting menus are studies in technical precision and Australian produce. Reservations open three months in advance and book within hours. For same-week access, a concierge is effectively essential.

Tetsuya's

Tetsuya Wakuda's legendary Kent Street restaurant has been a benchmark of Sydney fine dining for three decades. The Japanese-French degustation is one of the most refined experiences in Australia. Plan months ahead or ask your concierge to find cancellations.

Bentley Restaurant and Bar

Brent Savage's CBD restaurant is among Sydney's most decorated. The wine program is extraordinary and the food matches it — highly technical, ingredient-driven, genuinely exciting.

Aria

Matt Moran's flagship above the Opera House precinct combines exceptional produce with harbour views. Strong private dining for corporate entertainment.

Sydney's Best Wine Bars

Sydney's natural wine bar scene has exploded in five years. 10 William St in Paddington, Poly in Surry Hills, Maybe Sammy on the Rocks — neighbourhood institutions with fiercely loyal regulars and limited walk-in capacity.

Private Members Clubs

The Australian Club

Sydney's oldest and most traditional private club, founded 1838. Located in Macquarie Street. Membership by election — takes years. Serves the city's legal, political and business establishment.

The Union University and Schools Club

More accessible than The Australian Club but still prestigious. Reciprocal memberships with clubs worldwide — useful for international visitors.

Getting the Impossible Reservation

The best Sydney restaurants release cancellations on short notice, often via social media or to concierge services before the public. The difference between waiting three months and dining this Friday is simply knowing who to call.
