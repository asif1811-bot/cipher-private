We collect personal information you provide when creating an account, including your full name, email address, phone number, and payment details. We also collect information about your service requests and communications with Alina, our AI concierge.

We use your information to provide concierge services, process payments, send service updates and notifications, improve our AI systems, and communicate with you about your account.
