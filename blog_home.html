Managing Multiple Properties in Sydney: The Smart Owner's Guide

By Consiere·8 min read·Updated May 2026

The moment you own more than one property in Sydney, property management stops being an occasional inconvenience and becomes a part-time job. The regulatory requirements, tradespeople coordination, and maintenance scheduling compounds with every additional property.

The Tradespeople Problem

Finding reliable, qualified tradespeople in Sydney is one of the most consistently reported frustrations among property owners. Supply of skilled trades hasn't kept up with the construction boom. Good tradespeople are booked weeks ahead.

The solution is establishing relationships before you need them urgently. A trusted plumber, electrician, handyman, and painter — known and vetted with your properties on their books — transforms emergency maintenance from crisis to routine.

Preventive Maintenance Schedule

  • Annually: Pest inspection, roof inspection, gutters cleaned, smoke alarms tested (legally required), hot water system serviced
  • Every 2 years: Exterior repaint if rendered, garden maintenance, external drainage checked
  • Every 5 years: Internal repaint if tenanted, carpet assessment, bathroom seals
  • On vacancy: Professional clean, touch-up paint, carpet steam clean, all appliances tested

NSW Regulatory Compliance

  • Smoke alarms: Interconnected photoelectric alarms in all bedrooms and hallways — deadline has passed, all properties must comply
  • Swimming pools: CPR signage, compliant fencing, barrier certificate
  • Loose-fill asbestos: Properties built before 1985 may require testing
  • Minimum standards: All rental properties must meet habitability standards

When to Delegate

Most property owners find the break-even point at 2-3 properties. Beyond that, the time cost of self-management typically exceeds the cost of professional support. The question is not whether you can manage the properties yourself — it's whether that's the highest-value use of your time.
