CONSIERE VENDOR PARTNER AGREEMENT

This agreement is between Cipher Concierge Group Pty Ltd ABN [pending registration], trading as Consiere (“Consiere”), and the vendor business named in this application (“Vendor”).

1. Commission & Fees

1.1 Consiere operates on a 10% commission model. For every client lead fulfilled by the Vendor through the Consiere platform, Consiere retains 10% of the total invoice amount as its service fee.
1.2 The commission is calculated on the full invoice amount (GST inclusive where applicable).
1.3 The Vendor agrees that commission is payable on all bookings and services fulfilled through any Consiere referral, including follow-on bookings from the same client within 90 days of the initial referral.

2. Payment Terms

2.1 Client deposit: A $20 AUD booking deposit is collected by Consiere from the client to confirm each booking. This deposit is applied against the commission owed.
2.2 Vendor owes Consiere: Where the 10% commission exceeds the $20 deposit (i.e. invoice total exceeds $200), the Vendor is required to pay the outstanding commission balance to Consiere within 14 calendar days of the service delivery date. Failure to pay within this period may result in suspension from the Consiere vendor network.
2.3 Consiere owes Vendor: Where the $20 deposit collected exceeds the 10% commission (i.e. invoice total is less than $200), Consiere will refund the difference to the Vendor within 7 business days of the bill being submitted and verified through the Vendor Portal.
2.4 All payments between Consiere and Vendors are settled via bank transfer to the account details registered in the Vendor Portal.

3. Lead Exclusivity & Client Contact

3.1 The Vendor agrees not to contact Consiere clients directly for the purpose of soliciting business outside the Consiere platform for a period of 12 months from the date of initial introduction.
3.2 All client communication regarding bookings must be conducted through or with the knowledge of Consiere. The Vendor must not provide clients with direct contact details until the booking is confirmed through Consiere.
3.3 Breach of this clause may result in immediate removal from the Consiere vendor network and a claim for damages equal to 12 months' projected commission.

4. Service Standards

4.1 The Vendor agrees to maintain the highest standard of service commensurate with Consiere's brand positioning as a premium concierge service.
4.2 The Vendor must respond to all Consiere service requests within 2 hours during business hours (8am–8pm AEST Monday–Saturday).
4.3 The Vendor must honour all quotes submitted through the Consiere Vendor Portal for a minimum of 48 hours.
4.4 If the Vendor is unable to fulfil a confirmed booking, the Vendor must notify Consiere at least 24 hours in advance (or immediately in emergencies) and cooperate with finding an alternative solution for the client.

5. Insurance & Compliance

5.1 The Vendor warrants that it holds all necessary licences, registrations, and insurance required to provide its services lawfully in Australia, including but not limited to public liability insurance of no less than $5 million.
5.2 The Vendor agrees to provide Consiere with evidence of insurance and relevant licences upon request within 5 business days.
5.3 The Vendor shall comply with all applicable Australian laws and regulations, including consumer protection laws and privacy legislation.

6. Confidentiality

6.1 The Vendor agrees to keep all client information confidential and to use it solely for the purpose of fulfilling bookings made through Consiere.
6.2 The Vendor shall not disclose any client information to third parties without the prior written consent of Consiere, except as required by law.
6.3 The Vendor agrees to comply with the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles in handling all personal information received through the Consiere platform.

7. Termination

7.1 Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice to the other party.
7.2 Consiere may terminate this agreement immediately and without notice if the Vendor: (a) breaches any material term of this agreement; (b) engages in conduct that damages the reputation of Consiere; (c) fails to maintain required licences or insurance; or (d) becomes insolvent or enters administration.
7.3 Upon termination, any outstanding commission payments remain due and payable in accordance with the payment terms in clause 2.

8. Dispute Resolution

8.1 In the event of a dispute, both parties agree to first attempt resolution through good faith negotiation.
8.2 If unresolved within 14 days, disputes shall be referred to mediation before any legal proceedings are commenced.
8.3 This agreement is governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia.

9. Acknowledgements

By signing below, the Vendor acknowledges that they have read, understood, and agree to be bound by this Vendor Partner Agreement in its entirety.
